About the initiative

A vision for the future of LSC-Tomball

Introduced in Spring 2025, LSC-Tomball 3.0 is a strategic initiative focused on shaping the college's future through three complementary priorities.

As part of the Education Design Lab Reimagining Community Colleges Design Challenge, LSC-Tomball is using human-centered design to explore learner-centered, skills-based education and respond to regional workforce needs.

The work brings together students, faculty, staff, employers, and community partners.

Strategic priorities

Three strategies, one shared future

Near-term collaboration and long-term transformation, designed to support more learners and strengthen the regional community.

01
Short-term

249 Corridor of Opportunity

Team 249 focuses on creating opportunities through stronger partnerships between education, employers, and the surrounding community.

02
Mid-term

Community College Center for Neurodiversity Success

Exploring more inclusive experiences, resources, and pathways that support neurodivergent learners.

03
Long-term

Transferable Skills Benchmark · GPA+

Exploring approaches to recognizing and communicating the durable and transferable skills students build throughout their education.
