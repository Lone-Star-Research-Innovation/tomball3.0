Overview

About GPA+

GPA+, the Transferable Skills Benchmark, is the long-term strategy of Growing LSC-Tomball 3.0.

Goal Statement

To create a revolutionary measure that provides students with the ability to clearly demonstrate to potential employers the transferable skills they have learned, in addition to their grade point average.

The job-seeking landscape is changing, with more employers focusing on competencies such as critical thinking, collaboration, and communication. Lone Star College students will have a distinct advantage if they can present tangible evidence of the competencies they possess.

According to NACE's Job Outlook 2025 report, employers have decreased their use of the GPA as a screening tool, from 75% in 2019 to 46% in the latest report. The latest report also states that academic major and “demonstrated proficiency in competencies” replace GPA. Companies that do screen for GPA also report that a “candidate's proficiency in competencies” is a factor they consider in the hiring process. Similarly, LinkedIn reports that recruiting leaders will be prioritizing soft skills, stating that 69% of US executives say they plan to prioritize hiring candidates with soft skills.

Problems GPA+ Addresses

  1. GPA alone does not provide employers with evidence of the professional competencies they are seeking in college graduates.
  2. Employers are increasingly basing hiring decisions on professional competencies rather than GPA.
  3. LSC does not provide a tangible way for students to demonstrate to employers the professional competencies they have gained.
  4. Employers are developing their own mechanisms to gauge competencies.

Source: Growing LSC-Tomball 3.0 strategic plan, page 9.
