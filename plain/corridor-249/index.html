249 Corridor of Opportunity | Team 249

Short-Term Strategy: Three LSC locations, one corridor of opportunity along SH-249.

On this page: About | Framework | Progress | Resources | Other Strategies

About the Corridor

Goal Statement

To harness the geographic proximity of LSC-University Park, LSC-Tomball, and the LSC-Magnolia Center, all located along SH-249, and collectively impact access, success, and post-graduation success of students along the corridor.

The map shows the clear potential to create synergy along SH-249. Early indicators of that potential include co-marketing, advisor cross-training, shared course schedules, and collaboration on music and drama production schedules.

College 3.0 Framework

College 1.0: Expand Access

Exchange the LSC-Creekside and LSC-Magnolia Centers with LSC-Montgomery.

College 2.0: Collaborate for Success

Collaborate with LSC-University Park to anchor and create the 249 Corridor of Opportunity.

College 3.0 (where we're going): Engage Corridor Employers

Engage 249 corridor employers for improved opportunities for students.

Source: Growing LSC-Tomball 3.0 strategic plan (PDF), page 5.
