About LSC-Tomball 3.0

Introduced in Spring 2025, LSC-Tomball 3.0 encompasses a strategic plan with objectives and goals to reach that vision. Three primary strategies (short-term, mid-term, and long-term) are the basis of the plan.

To accomplish our goals, LSC-Tomball joined the Reimagining Community Colleges Design Challenge, a national initiative led by Education Design Lab (the Lab).

The challenge supports institutions in crafting their strategic transformation plans using the Lab's human-centered design approach. The project focuses on advancing learner-centered, skills-based education to align with the future of work and fill regional skills gaps needed to fuel economic prosperity.

In this video, LSC-Tomball President Dr. Lee Ann Nutt explains the collaboration.

Read more from Dr. Nutt: What Higher Ed Can Learn From the Curb Cut Effect (The EvoLLLution, July 2026).

Three Strategies

Each strategy moves from a familiar starting point (1.0) to a new model of student success (3.0).

Short-Term: 249 Corridor of Opportunity

To harness the geographic proximity of LSC-University Park, LSC-Tomball, and the LSC-Magnolia Center, all located along SH-249, and collectively impact access, success, and post-graduation success of students along the corridor.

Mid-Term: Institute for Neurodiversity in Academic and Career Success

To create the Institute for Neurodiversity in Academic and Career Success (INACS), a 501(c)3 non-profit organization dedicated to dramatically increasing access to higher education, improving completion rates, and enhancing post-graduation outcomes for neurodiverse individuals.

Long-Term: GPA+ (Transferable Skills Benchmark)

To create a revolutionary measure that provides students with the ability to clearly demonstrate to potential employers the transferable skills they have learned, in addition to their grade point average.
