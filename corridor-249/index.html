About the Corridor

Goal Statement

To harness the geographic proximity of LSC-University Park, LSC-Tomball, and the LSC-Magnolia Center, all located along SH-249, and collectively impact access, success, and post-graduation success of students along the corridor.

The map shows the clear potential to create synergy along SH-249. Early indicators of that potential include co-marketing, advisor cross-training, shared course schedules, and collaboration on music and drama production schedules.
